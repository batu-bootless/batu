On examination, the abdomen is diffusely tender. Laparotomy reveals bilateral renal infarcts and multiple segments of necrosis in the small bowel. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

  • A. Bacterial endocarditis

  • D. Polyarteritis nodosa
